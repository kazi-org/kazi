## T79.1: Bound total dispatches across model rungs

### Objective

A configured total allowance stops the invocation before launching worker three,
even if the ladder would give another rung a fresh local allowance.

### Scope

- `lib/kazi/budget.ex`
- `lib/kazi/goal/loader.ex`
- `lib/kazi/authoring.ex`
- `lib/kazi/loop/budget.ex`
- `lib/kazi/loop.ex`
- `lib/kazi/loop/ladder.ex`
- `test/kazi/loop/budget_test.exs`
- `test/kazi/loop/escalation_ladder_test.exs`
- `test/kazi/authoring_test.exs`
- New loader coverage in `test/kazi/goal/total_dispatch_budget_test.exs`

Out of scope: model selection, replacing per-rung semantics, cross-invocation
budgets, or estimating actual provider cost from launch counts.

### Acceptance criteria

Parse proposed `budget.max_total_dispatches` as a positive integer or absent;
reject zero, negative, boolean, string and fractional values. Absent preserves
existing behavior. Round-trip the same field through proposal authoring and
persistence. Use the invocation's cumulative dispatch counter, not the
ladder-relative counter. Check admission before launch. Failed/timeout attempts
consume the allowance; observation-only ticks do not.

Allow final verification to converge after the last permissible attempt. If it
still fails, terminate with the total-dispatch budget reason and preserve the
current candidate and evidence. Keep per-rung budgets unchanged. Counter resets
must not occur on rung transitions; a new invocation is separately budgeted.

Genuine red: a fake two-rung harness with a total limit of two presently launches
again or lacks the field. Record that failure. After implementation the third
launch sentinel never appears. Disable the cumulative gate to reproduce failure.
Also cover success on attempt two, first launch error, no-op ticks and absent field.
